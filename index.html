FAQ

Common questions

How much does window replacement cost?

It depends on how many windows, their size and style, and what the openings need, so we measure on site and give you a quote before anything starts.

How long does an install take?

Most homes are done in a day or two, depending on how many windows and doors are going in.

Do you replace front and patio doors too?

Yes. Entry doors, sidelites, patio and sliding doors, installed and sealed.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Tinaj Construction is licensed, insured and covered by WSIB.

Do you do commercial work?

Yes, for storefronts, offices and multi-unit buildings, as a direct hire or a subcontractor.
